====================================================================
HOW TO INSTALL ASCENT ON YOUR CHROMEBOOK (WITH SEARCH BAR SUPPORT)
====================================================================

METHOD 1: USING YOUR `ascent-sheet.html`, `manifest.json`, `background.js` & `sw.js` FILES
1. Open the Chromebook "Files" app and create a new folder named `Ascent`.
2. Move `ascent-sheet.html`, `manifest.json`, `background.js`, and `sw.js` (or extract this entire zip) into that `Ascent` folder.
   (Make sure none of the files have `(1)` in their name, e.g. `manifest.json`, NOT `manifest (1).json`).
3. Open Chrome, type `chrome://extensions` in the search bar, and press Enter.
4. Turn ON the "Developer mode" switch in the top-right corner.
5. Click "Load unpacked" (top-left button) and select your `Ascent` folder.
6. Done! Now you can:
   - Type `ascent` + Space + Enter in the Chrome search bar (address bar) to open Ascent as a standalone Chromebook window!
   - Or click the puzzle icon in Chrome's toolbar, pin "Ascent — 2027 Tracker", and click it anytime.

METHOD 2: INSTALL DIRECTLY FROM CHROME'S TOP BAR / MENU ON CHROMEBOOK
1. Open `ascent-sheet.html` in Chrome on your Chromebook.
2. Click the three dots (⋮) at the top-right of Chrome -> "Save and share" (or "More tools") -> "Install page as app..." (or "Create shortcut..." -> check "Open as window").
3. Ascent will now appear on your Chromebook Shelf and Launcher!
